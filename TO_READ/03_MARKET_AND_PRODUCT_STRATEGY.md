# 03 — Market & Product Strategy

## Target Audience

### Primary ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Role** | Pedagogical Inspector, University Lecturer, Master's Student |
| **Language** | French-fluent (French is official language of Burkina Faso administration) |
| **Domain** | Education research — specifically teaching German as a foreign language (FLE/FAD) |
| **Organization** | Ministry of Fundamental Education (EIF), University of Ouagadougou, UEMAF member states |
| **Pain Point** | Academic research methodology is time-consuming; questionnaire design, literature review, and bibliography formatting take disproportionate effort |
| **Budget** | Institutional (university lab grants, ministry research budgets, World Bank/FAFO funded programs) |
| **Decision Process** | Department head or lab director recommends tools to researchers; peer approval matters more than personal preference |

### Secondary Audiences

| Segment | Need | Willingness to Pay |
|---------|------|--------------------|
| German teachers in Burkina Faso | Classroom action research tools | Low (individual, self-funded) |
| Francophone African countries expanding German education | Similar workflow adapted to local curriculum | Medium (institutional) |
| European exchange partners (Goethe-Institut WAHO) | Monitoring & evaluation tools for teacher training | High (grant-funded) |
| Other subject areas (English, Science didactics) | Adapted workflow for their domain | Medium-High (proven demand if German tool works well) |

## Market Research

### Existing Alternatives

| Alternative | What It Does | Gap vs Dagara |
|-------------|-------------|---------------|
| **Manual research** | Pen/paper, Google Scholar, Word documents | Slow, error-prone, no structure guidance |
| **Zotero / Mendeley** | Reference management, citation formatting | No research workflow guidance, no AI generation |
| **Google Scholar** | Literature search | No synthesis, no questionnaire builder, no analysis |
| **SurveyMonkey / KoboToolbox** | Survey creation & data collection | No domain expertise, no AI analysis aligned to hypotheses |
| **General AI (ChatGPT/Claude)** | Answer questions, draft text | No project continuity, no structured output, no research methodology built-in |
| **SPSS / R Studio** | Statistical analysis | Requires statistical expertise; steep learning curve |

### Competitors (Direct)

None found. No product currently combines:
1. Academic research workflow guidance
2. AI-powered content generation
3. Domain-specific for German didactics in Francophone Africa

This is a **blue ocean** niche — small market but essentially zero direct competition.

### Indirect Competitors

- **Academic consultants** — Individual consultants who write methodology sections for researchers (charge $500–$5000 per thesis)
- **University workshops** — Methodology training sessions (free but infrequent)
- **Thesis coaching services** — Personal tutoring ($20–$100/hour)

### Market Expectations

From the ICP's perspective:
- Research tools must produce **publishable-quality output** — not just drafts.
- Tools must respect **academic rigor** — no fabricated sources, no hallucinated statistics.
- Tools must integrate with **existing workflows** — not replace them.
- Tools must be **mobile-friendly** — fieldwork happens away from desktop.
- Tools must be **affordable** — researcher salaries in West Africa are low; institutional procurement is slow.

### Differentiation Opportunities

1. **Domain specificity** — Only product targeting German didactics in Burkina Faso. Every prompt, every template, every suggestion is contextualized.
2. **End-to-end workflow** — From literature review to final bibliography, all in one tool. Most alternatives handle only one stage.
3. **Field-ready** — Designed for use on tablets/phones in rural schools, not just office desktops.
4. **Multi-agent architecture potential** — Future version could have specialized agents: literature agent, methodology agent, analysis agent, each optimized for their domain.

## Positioning Statement

> **For** French-speaking educational researchers and inspectors in Burkina Faso conducting academic research on German language teaching,
> **Dagara Germanist AI** is the **first AI-powered research assistant** designed specifically for the didactique de l'allemand workflow,
> **Unlike** generic AI tools or manual research processes,
> **It provides** structured guidance through every stage of academic research — from literature review to final bibliography — generating publication-quality questionnaires, analyses, and citations in under five minutes.

### One-Sentence Value Proposition

> "Structurez votre recherche sur l'enseignement de l'allemand au Burkina Faso avec une IA spécialisée qui rédige vos questionnaires, analyse vos données et génère votre bibliographie en quelques clics."

### Core Benefits

1. **Time savings** — Reduce questionnaire design from days to minutes. Bibliography generation eliminates manual APA formatting.
2. **Research quality** — AI suggestions grounded in actual didactic theory (action approach, socio-constructivism, etc.), not generic templates.
3. **Methodological rigor** — Structured five-phase workflow ensures completeness. Every hypothesis maps to research questions which map to data collection tools.
4. **Accessibility** — No technical expertise required. Upload PDFs, get analysis. No SPSS/R knowledge needed.
5. **Field-ready** — Works on Android phones/tablets for classroom observations and teacher interviews.

### Strongest Differentiator

**Domain expertise baked into every interaction.** While ChatGPT can write a questionnaire about anything, Dagara understands APC (Approche Par Compétences), class composition in Burkinabè schools, the specific textbook series used (Ihr und Wir, Wege), and the regulatory framework of the Burkina Faso education system. This specificity is what makes it valuable versus a generic tool.

## Monetization Hypotheses

### Recommended Model: **Freemium B2B with Institutional Licensing**

| Tier | Price | Features | Target |
|------|-------|----------|--------|
| **Free** | $0 | 5 AI generations/month, 2 projects, basic export (.doc) | Individual students, pilot testing |
| **Individual Pro** | $5/month | Unlimited generations, unlimited projects, PDF export, priority AI, chatbot history | Active researchers, PhD candidates |
| **Institutional** | Custom ($20–$50/user/year) | Everything in Pro + team collaboration, admin dashboard, API access, custom branding, training sessions | Universities, ministries, Goethe-Institut |

### Pricing Rationale

- $5/month aligns with subscription norms in emerging markets (Spotify Nigeria ₦599/month ≈ $0.40; Netflix Kenya KES 500/month ≈ $3.75).
- Institutional licensing reflects standard EdTech pricing: $20–50/user/year for classroom/deployment licenses.
- Free tier limited enough to prevent abuse but generous enough for genuine exploration.

### Acquisition Strategy

1. **Direct institutional outreach** — Present at EIF pedagogy seminars, university department meetings. Offer free institutional trials to 3-5 departments.
2. **Conference presence** — Present results at ACTALEF (Association pour la Promotion des Langues Étrangères Enseignées en Afrique Francophone), Goethe-Institut events.
3. **Partnership with Goethe-Institut WAHO** — They actively promote German education in Burkina Faso. Integration would give immediate credibility and distribution.
4. **Content marketing** — Publish blog posts in French on research methodology tips for German didactics. Drive organic traffic to app.
5. **Referral program** — Existing users refer colleagues → extended free trials.
